# Competitor review (20 Oct 2026)

**Method:**
- App Store ratings for the UK and US came from Apple's public API.
- Complaints came from roughly 1,000 recent US and UK App Store reviews per app; the 1–2★ reviews were keyword-tagged.
- Trustpilot was used where its pages loaded.
- Google Play and Reddit could not be fetched.
- Several prices come from aggregators and are marked as unverified.

## The landscape
- **Content and sleep incumbents** have large audiences and trusted libraries, but no personalisation:
  - Calm: 326K UK ratings
  - Headspace: 355K
  - CBeebies Storytime: 67K, free
  - Moshi: 19.6K UK / 70K US
  - Epic: 15K UK / 519K US
  - Yoto: 12.5K UK / 30K US
  - Tonies: 3.3K UK / 8.4K US
- **AI personalisers are numerous, with almost no traction:**
  - Oscar Stories, the best known, has **5 UK ratings (2.8★)** and 56 US ratings.
  - PerfectTales has 0 ratings, and so does StoryBee's iOS app.
  - A UK search for "bedtime stories ai" returns about 28 apps, nearly all with 0–12 ratings.
- **The free big-tech threat is adult-gated:**
  - Google Gemini Storybook is free, 10 illustrated pages, 18+ only.
  - Meta StoryKit has been in beta in some countries since July 2026.

## Top 10 recurring complaints
1. **Billing:** trial traps, charges after cancelling, unreachable support (Moshi, Storybook, Epic, Scarlett Panda).
2. **Paywall shock:** "free" apps that aren't.
3. **Currency:** confusing coins, credits and tokens (Oscar, Fable).
4. **Repetition:** content runs out or repeats (Storybook, Moshi, Tonies).
5. **Narration:** narrators changed, too animated, or volume jumping (Moshi, Storybook).
6. **Scary content at bedtime:** Moshi, Zoog, and AI outputs going dark.
7. **Bedtime failures:** Wi-Fi drops, logouts, generation timeouts.
8. **Weak bedtime controls:** no lock after the sleep timer, no "stop at end of chapter", no dark mode.
9. **No family support:** no Family Sharing or multi-child plans.
10. **AI quality and distrust:** character drift, generic or preachy text, anti-AI sentiment.

## White space
1. A persistent world that remembers, at scale.
2. A locked visual identity across stories, using an avatar builder rather than photos.
3. AI personalisation combined with real sleep craft (wind-down narration, dimming, fade to ambient).
4. Narrator stability as a promise.
5. Parent-first bedtime controls (lights-out lock, no autoplay, end at chapter).
6. Honest billing as a brand pillar.
7. A curated, human-edited library alongside AI, clearly labelled.
8. Word-synced read-along in AI books.
9. A UK-native product: British voices, GBP pricing, the ICO Children's Code.
10. Gentle safety for anxious children: avoid lists, no late-story peril.

## Pricing anchors
- **AI subscriptions:** $3–15/mo; annual $40–100; outlier Fable at $275/yr.
- **UK sleep and content annual plans:** £35–60. Family libraries: £75–80.
- **Print:** £20–50 per book.
- **Recommendation:** £6.99/mo or £49.99/yr core; £9.99/mo or £79.99/yr Plus, with all children included. Avoid weekly plans and coins.
